# Bakebuild
All my bakebuild files are here that I made for hackclub bakebuild.
<br>
## What did I make?
I made a fish, dog, and an Orpheus cookie cutter. Orpheus is one of the Hackclub mascots.
<br>
## How did I make?
I used onshape to make the 3D model part, and canva to do the sketches. To make the Orph cookie cutter, I traced around a sticker. The dog one, I sketched around a cartoon image of a dog. Then, for the fish one I made it completely by myself by combining an oval and triangle shape.
<br>
## Instructions
1. Read the disclaimer below, and check your filament brand and type.
2. Print the cookie cutters out. Chose any colour you want, but I think black or white would look the best.
3. Wait for the plastic to cool, then remove it from your printbed.
4. Make your own cookie dough!
5. Put about 2 layers of cling wrap over the cookie cutter.
6. Need your dough till it's the right thickness, and then push down on the cookie cutter, shaking it slightly.
7. Bake your cookies.
8. Decorate them, and enjoy!
<br>
**Disclaimer**
Print at your own risk. By printing this, you agree I am not responsible for any incidents that occur. Make sure you're careful what filament your using, and that it's food safe. I reccomend putting a layer of cling wrap on your cutter so it doesn't cause any microplastics to enter your food.
